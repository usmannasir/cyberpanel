Change the role

  • Controller: you manage your other servers from this one. It needs a Multi-Server Manager licence. Get a licence for this server
  • Managed server: a controller that you allow can manage this server. No licence is needed here.
  • Off: Multi-Server Manager is not used on this server.

A server has only one role. To switch between controller and managed server, first remove its servers (on a controller) or end its controllers (on a managed server).

Choosing Off pauses Multi-Server Manager but keeps its connections: choose the same role again and they work as before. To end a connection for good, remove the server on the controller's Managed servers page, or end the link on the managed server's Controllers page.
