phpMyAdmin OLS fix (v2.5.5-dev)
================================

Problem
-------
On OpenLiteSpeed :8090, CyberPanel proxied all traffic to Django/gunicorn.
Django serve_phpmyadmin served .php as static files (5804-byte download).

Fix (automatic on install/upgrade)
----------------------------------
plogical/cyberpanelOlsPhpmyadmin.py writes /usr/local/lsws/conf/vhosts/CyberPanel/vhost.conf
with explicit /phpmyadmin/ and /snappymail/ contexts + lsapi:cyberpanelphp scripthandler.

Hooks:
- install/install.py -> ensureCyberPanelPhpmyadminOls() after setupLSCPDDaemon
- plogical/upgrade.py -> after download_install_phpmyadmin and post-upgrade service verify

Manual repair
-------------
sudo bash /usr/local/CyberCP/fix-phpmyadmin.sh
# or
sudo bash /usr/local/CyberCP/CPScripts/fix-cyberpanel-phpmyadmin-ols.sh --restart

Verify
------
curl -skI https://127.0.0.1:8090/phpmyadmin/phpmyadminsignin.php | grep -i x-powered-by
POST must be < 5000 bytes (not 5804).
